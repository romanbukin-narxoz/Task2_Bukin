# Sample data
These 10 English email-like texts were created artificially for a software smoke test.
They contain five spam and five ham messages and no personal information.
They are distributed under the project MIT license. They are not a real email corpus.
No accuracy, generalisation or significance claim may be based on this sample.
CSV schema: id (unique identifier), text (nonempty message), label (ham or spam).
Duplicate text after lowercasing and whitespace normalisation is rejected.

version, licence, language, class counts, cleaning and deduplication procedure in docs/dataset.md.
